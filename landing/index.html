Features

Built native. Built private.

⚡

Native background capture

Counts keystrokes, clicks, scrolls, cursor distance and shortcut chords via WM_INPUT, CGEventTap and XInput2 — with minimal CPU overhead.

🔒

Strict local privacy

Never logs typed characters, passwords, clipboard, window titles or coordinates. Only aggregate key codes and counts, stored in local SQLite.

👻

Tray & Stealth mode

Live in the tray or vanish completely. Wake stealth mode anytime with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T — or just relaunch the app.

📊

Rich analytics

Live WPM/CPM, mouse odometer, streaks, keyboard heatmap & treemap, 365-day calendar, hourly trends and per-app attribution.

⌨️

Typing test & shortcuts

Speed tests in English, فارسی and Code, plus a platform-aware shortcut atlas and 7 styled PNG report cards to share your stats.

🌍

Multilingual & themed

English and Persian (full RTL + Jalali calendar) plus 7 more languages. Light/dark modes, 4 palettes, 80–150% UI scaling, CSV/JSON export.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your machine.

TypeCount has no account, no analytics SDK and no network calls for your activity. Everything lives in typecount.sqlite3 on your disk — export it, back it up, or merge databases across machines whenever you like.

✕ Never stored

  • Typed characters & passwords
  • Clipboard contents
  • Window titles & screen coordinates
  • Anything sent to a server

✓ Stored locally

  • Aggregate key codes & click counts
  • Scroll steps & cursor distance
  • Optional foreground app names
  • Daily totals in SQLite
